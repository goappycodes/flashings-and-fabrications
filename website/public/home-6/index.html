Flashings & Fabrication Services makes the flashings, gutters, louvres and folded parts that finish a building, right down to the small brackets and soakers that hold a roof together.

Everything is cut, punched, folded and powder coated under one roof on Harwood Road, Littlehampton. Roofers, cladding contractors and main contractors come to us because we make exactly what the drawing says, small runs included, and the people who make it pick up the phone.

Flashings & trims

Folded to the profile on your drawing in steel or aluminium, mill finish, galvanised or powder coated. Long lengths come off the brake in one piece.

  • Drip, verge and eaves flashings

  • Soakers, aprons and upstands
  • Angles, closures and trims
  • Bullnoses and curved flashings

Punch, fold and coat under one roof.

Most of what we make doesn't leave the building until it's finished. That keeps lead times short, colours consistent and quality in our own hands.

CNC

Turret punch

Your drawing or 3D model goes straight to the machine, which punches holes, slots and shapes the same way every time. Ideal for perforated panels, louvre blades and repeat brackets.

4m

Press brakes

Four-metre beds fold full-length copings, gutters and flashings in one piece, so there are fewer joints to seal on site.

RAL

Powder coating oven

A large oven takes long lengths and big batches, so a whole job is coated together in the colour you specify and comes out matching.
