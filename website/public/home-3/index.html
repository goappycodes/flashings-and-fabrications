We make the metal details that finish a building and keep the weather out. For roofing contractors, cladding installers and main contractors across the UK.

Every piece is made to order from your drawing, or your sketch. From a single 300 mm soaker to a run of 4-metre powder-coated copings.

The turret punch, press brakes and coating line all sit in our own works, so accuracy, finish and lead time stay in our hands. And when you ring, you speak to the people making it.

Punch. Fold. Coat.
All under one roof.

Most of what we make never leaves our Littlehampton works until it's finished. Three machines do the heavy lifting, and they're among the largest of their kind in the region.

CNC turret punch

Your drawing goes straight from the screen to the sheet. Holes, slots, notches and cut-outs are punched automatically, so the hundredth part matches the first.

Works from
CAD files and 3D models
Good for
Perforated panels, louvre blades, brackets

4-metre press brakes

Long beds mean long, straight copings, cappings and gutters in a single length. Fewer joints to seal on site, and a cleaner line on the building.

Bed length
4,000 mm
Good for
Copings, cappings, gutters, trays

Large-format coating oven

A bigger oven costs more to run, but it lets us coat long lengths and large assemblies in one go. Colour-matched, cured and checked before anything leaves.
