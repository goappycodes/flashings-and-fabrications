Our workshop

Punching, folding and powder coating, all under one roof in Littlehampton.

Holes, slots and cut-outs, punched straight from your drawing.

Your CAD file goes from the screen to the sheet. Perforated panels, louvre blades and brackets come out identical, whether you need ten or a thousand.

Copings, cappings and gutters folded in one long length.

Four-metre beds mean fewer joints to seal on site and a cleaner line on the building. Short runs and awkward profiles are just as welcome.

A large oven, so long lengths and big assemblies are coated in-house.

Any RAL colour, matt to gloss, matched to your fascias and cappings. Colour and timing never depend on someone else's queue.

A specialist fabricator, not a stockist. Set up for awkward sizes, short runs and colour-matched finishes, as well as the big repeat orders.
